About Us

Creativity Meets Strategy.

“In today’s fast-paced digital world, attention is everything. A great visual doesn’t just make people stop scrolling; it makes them understand, connect, and take action.”

Muhammad Hakeem is the founder of Videos2Grow, a video editor and motion designer with experience dating back to 2016. Over the years he has worked across video production, motion graphics, animation, and digital content creation, expanding into AI-powered production and creative marketing.

Years of creating animations taught him that effective marketing goes beyond highlighting product benefits. It starts with understanding the audience's challenges and positioning your business as the solution.
